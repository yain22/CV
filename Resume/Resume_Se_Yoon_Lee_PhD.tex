\cvsection{Research Profile}
Statistician combining a strong theoretical foundation in causal inference, semiparametric efficiency theory, and Bayesian analysis with professional experience at Big Pharma (AbbVie, Johnson \& Johnson, and Amgen). Strong publication record in leading statistics/biostatistics journals and ML/AI conference proceedings.
\par\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{Research areas:} Causal inference; semiparametric efficiency; external controls and data borrowing; Bayesian analysis and theory; survival analysis; machine learning/artificial intelligence.
